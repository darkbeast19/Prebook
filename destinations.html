Best Time to Visit Kashmir

🌸

Spring (Mar–May)

Tulip gardens, blossoming orchards, mild weather. The most colourful season.

☀️

Summer (Jun–Aug)

Pleasant 20–30°C, all routes open, peak tourist season. Book early!

🍂

Autumn (Sep–Nov)

Golden Chinar leaves, saffron harvest, fewer crowds. A photographer's dream.

❄️

Winter (Dec–Feb)

Skiing in Gulmarg, snow-covered landscapes, cosy houseboat experience.

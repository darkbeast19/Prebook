Who We Are

Born in the Valley, Built for Travellers

Prebook Holidays was founded in 2009 by a team of local Kashmiri travel enthusiasts who believed that visitors deserved more than a generic "Kashmir package" — they deserved an experience that felt authentic, personal and transformative.

Today, operating under the brand Prebook Holidays, we serve thousands of travellers each year from our offices in New Delhi, Pune and Gwalior — but our heart remains in the Valley.

Every member of our ground team is a local Kashmiri — from our expert guides to our houseboat hosts. This means you get insider access to places, stories and experiences that no guidebook can offer.
